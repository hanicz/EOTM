Record a stock grant and the vesting schedule is worked out for you — the shares split as evenly as whole shares allow, so no vest is more than one share off any other. Each vest is valued at the closing price on its own date and converted to forint at that day's MNB rate, then taxed the Hungarian way: 89% of the value is the tax base, carrying 13% szocho and 15% szja. Vests that have not happened yet are marked as projected and priced at the latest close and rate available, held flat — they are an estimate, not a forecast.
