A STAR pays out the appreciation, not the whole share — granted at an FMV of 10 and worth 11 today, each unit is worth 1. The value is not public, so you type in what you were last told it is worth. The schedule is worked out for you: a quarter of the grant vests on the first anniversary of the vesting commencement date and the rest lands in equal quarterly instalments, so a four-year grant is a 25% cliff and then a sixteenth every quarter. Commencement dates only ever fall on 8 January, 8 April, 8 July or 8 October, so whatever date you enter is moved forward to the next of them. Every instalment — vested or not — is valued at today's spread and today's MNB rate, then taxed the Hungarian way: 89% of the value is the tax base, carrying 13% szocho and 15% szja. It answers what the grant is worth if you cashed out today, not what any past instalment was actually worth on its own date.
