Everything you are paid on top of the base salary — SZÉP card, bonus, phone, insurance. The base comes from the salary period you are on now, so you only enter the extras. Each one is either a monthly amount or a share of the annual base, and its tax treatment decides what actually lands: taxed as salary loses 15% income tax and 18.5% social contribution, tax-free and already-net items keep their full value.
