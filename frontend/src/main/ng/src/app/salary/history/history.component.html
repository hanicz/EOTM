Enter what you earned and over which period, either as a monthly or an annual figure — the other one is worked out for you. Net pay is calculated with Hungarian employee deductions: 15% personal income tax and 18.5% social contribution, less the family allowance for the dependants you enter. The raise column compares each net monthly figure with the period before it, so it shows what actually landed in your pocket.
