Valorisation restates your whole career at the wage level of the year before you retire, so the answer sits at today’s prices but at future wage levels — that is why it can land well above pensions being paid today. It is modelled as a steady national wage growth rather than the published year-by-year multipliers, and it compounds over the whole gap to your retirement year, so small changes to that rate move the answer a lot. Today’s 18.5% contribution and 15% tax rates are assumed to hold for your whole career. Past years come from your salary records, day-weighted within each calendar year. The replacement rate compares against a final net pay worked out with no dependants.
