Works out the Hungarian state pension (öregségi nyugdíj) from your salary history and the path you expect your pay to take. Every figure is in today’s forints — a result of 700,000 means what 700,000 buys now, not what the number will say in thirty years. Note that valorisation restates your whole career at the wage level of the year before you retire, so the answer sits at today’s prices but at future wage levels. That is why it can land well above pensions being paid today, and why the wage growth assumption below moves it so much.

Valorisation is modelled as a steady national wage growth rather than the published year-by-year multipliers, and it compounds over the whole gap to your retirement year, so small changes to that rate move the answer a lot. Today’s 18.5% contribution and 15% tax rates are assumed to hold for your whole career. Past years come from your salary records, day-weighted within each calendar year. The replacement rate compares against a final net pay worked out with no dependants.
